## Core arguments (recurring across posts)

- Founders misdiagnose problems as pricing/features/traffic when the real killer is UX friction
- Clarity beats beauty — decoration adds mental work, clarity removes it
- Real proof (screenshots, workflows, before/afters) beats marketing copy
- Design debt compounds — every "fix it later" is a tax on users that eventually causes churn
- Retainers beat hourly — speed and access matter more than billing model
- AI commoditizes pixel work, designers who own strategy and judgment survive
- Every post is actionable: what's broken, how to recognize it, how to fix it
