# Humbl Rule #2 — Rewrites

**Original (avoid 1:1 match):**
> Pretty websites get likes. Clear websites get demos booked.

---

## Short rewrites

1. Clarity books demos. Polish doesn't.
2. Pretty ≠ profitable.
3. Clean design, confused message = zero demos.
4. Compliments don't convert. Clarity does.
5. A pretty site that doesn't convert is just art.
6. Likes pay nothing. Clarity pays rent.
7. If it looks great and converts at 0%, it's broken.
8. Design for the click, not the compliment.

**Pick:** #8 — fits as a Humbl Rule, no parallel-structure tells.
