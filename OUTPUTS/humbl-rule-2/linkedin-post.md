# Humbl Rule #2 — LinkedIn Post

**Headline / Rule:** Design for the click, not the compliment.

---

## Final post body (scheduled Monday, May 11, 2026)

Your investor told you the new site looks great. Demo bookings stayed flat. The click doesn't care about your color palette.

What the click wants:
– A hero you can scan in 3 seconds
– Copy that names the actual problem in plain words
– One CTA, repeated across the page
– Social proof above the fold

Most founders measure the wrong thing. Compliments feel good. Your CEO friend, your designer cousin, the founder Twitter quote-tweet, the LinkedIn comments. None of them book the call.

I see this a lot. Founders ship a clean redesign, conversions drop, and they sit on the data for weeks because "the site feels premium now." The runway doesn't care how the site feels.

Open your analytics. Last 30 days. If traffic is fine and bookings are flat, your site has a clarity problem.
