# Content Idea: Website Pages CRO Series

**Logged:** May 2026  
**Status:** Idea — not started  
**Format:** LinkedIn post series (one post per page type) + possible long-form guide

---

## The Idea

A content series covering every standard startup website page — one post per page — breaking down the CRO-friendly blocks and elements each page needs, what most startups get wrong, and what to fix first.

## Page Types to Cover

- Hero / above the fold
- Pricing page
- Features page
- About page
- Careers page
- Blog / resources
- Contact page
- Case studies / testimonials page
- Waitlist / coming soon page
- 404 page (bonus)

## Post Format (per page)

Each post: screenshot or illustration of common bad version → what's broken → what a CRO-optimised version includes → the 3-5 blocks/elements every [page type] needs.

## Why This Works

- Repeatable format = easy to produce weekly
- Each post is highly shareable (every founder has one of these pages)
- Positions David as the "website guy" who knows every page, not just landing pages
- Builds toward a long-form guide: "The CRO blueprint for every page on your startup website"

## Possible CTA Per Post

"DM 'AUDIT' + your URL — I'll tell you which of these your page is missing."
