# Humbl Rule 01 — CTA

**The Rule:**
If your CTA says "Learn more", users will learn nothing.

---

**LinkedIn Copy:**

"Learn more" is the most popular CTA (call to action) on the internet and the most useless. It tells users nothing. Where am I going? What will I see? What do I get? Founders pick it because it feels neutral. That's the problem.

CTAs should remove hesitation!

Swap the label for what actually happens:
"Learn more" → "See how it works"
"Learn more" → "See what it costs"
"Learn more" → "See it in 2 minutes"
"Learn more" → "Join the waitlist"

The label should complete this sentence: "I want to ___."

If "learn more" doesn't complete it, cut it.

One word change & real difference in clicks.
