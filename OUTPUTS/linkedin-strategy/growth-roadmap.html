Phase A · 2026
Do the work. Document everything.
Get clients. Charge more. Make money. Run the redesign engine publicly. Every client win, every pricing decision, every hustle story goes on LinkedIn. Build the audience that gives you permission to teach later. The CRO-for-designers wedge is the entry point. The journey is the differentiator.
The bridge that makes Phase B work: CRO posts build Phase A authority. Journey posts — landing clients, raising rates, the Sydney origin story — build Phase B permission. Start both from day 1. Target ratio: 4 CRO/redesign posts per 1 journey post. The people who save the journey posts are exactly the people who buy the business-growth course in 2027.

Phase B · 2027 +
Teach designers how to do what you did.
Once you've documented the climb in public, you have the social proof to teach it. Not just CRO — the full picture. How to build a personal brand. How to grow on LinkedIn. How to get leads without chasing. How to charge $150/hr when everyone else is stuck at $50. You're not the best agency. You're the person who shows designers how to build one.
Note on audience overlap: Some followers will come for CRO tips and not care about building a business. That's fine — they buy freebies, share posts, and grow the list. The buyers of the Phase B course are a subset: the designers who followed the journey posts closely. The ratio is small (maybe 5–10% of total audience) but at 80K+ followers that's 4–8K potential students. That funds everything.

⚠ What causes this

Treating LinkedIn like a side projectClient work is urgent. LinkedIn is important. Urgent wins every time you don't protect the morning hours.
Newsletter launch delayedBeehiiv recommendations need subscribers to activate. Every month delayed is compounding lost. 3 months late = 6 months behind on list growth.
Journey pillar avoidedThe personal content feels vulnerable. That's exactly why it builds trust. Skipping it removes the entire foundation for Phase B.
No freebie systemLead magnets are the fastest list-building tool available. Without them the newsletter stays under 1K subs and the course launch math never works.
No client pipeline builtIf Augment pauses or a retainer ends, there's nothing to replace it. LinkedIn was the insurance policy. It didn't get built.

→ What fixes it

2 hours before client work — non-negotiable6–8 AM content block. Not "when I have time." Treat it exactly like a client retainer.
Newsletter live by June 1 — imperfect is fineFirst issue doesn't need to be good. It needs to exist. Ship the thing.
CRO Pocket Guide in one Sunday session8 principles. 8 pages. PDF. That unlocks the lead magnet engine. Total build time: 4 hours.
One journey post per week from day one"I just landed a $10K project from LinkedIn. Here's exactly how." That post will outperform every design breakdown you write.

⚠ What can still break this

Burning out from the volume of attentionGoing viral is energising and exhausting simultaneously. 350+ comments, 20 DMs/day, podcast requests. You need systems before this hits — not after. VA hired by month 4.
Course launched wrong — too cheap or too early$997 to 55K followers with a warm waitlist is the right price. Launch at $97 out of fear and you anchor the price low permanently. Launch to 5K followers and you leave $140K on the table waiting.
Studio demand swamps content timeWhen the client waitlist is 6 deep it's tempting to say yes to all of them. Content is the engine that created the waitlist. Protect it before protecting revenue.
Competition copies the playbookAt 100K+ followers, 3–5 others will enter the CRO-for-designers lane. The moat: the Sydney origin story is unreplicable. The documented journey (in real time, with real numbers) is unreplicable. Being 2 years ahead is unreplicable. Own those, hard.

→ What makes this the floor, not the ceiling

The origin story is a permanent moat"$200 in Sydney → $115K/mo teaching designers" is a story nobody else has. Told well, with real numbers, in public — it compounds. New designers find it years later and it still converts.
A newsletter at 185K subs is a business unto itselfThat asset is worth $1–2M as an acquisition. It funds course launches, book advances, and the community build with zero ad spend.
Teaching designers is a 10-year market100,000+ new designers enter the market every year. Every one of them will eventually face the same problem Alex has now. The market doesn't saturate.
Travel is the proof of concept, not just the reward"Running a $115K/mo education business from Bali, Prague, and Lisbon" is the best-performing content David can make in 2028. It validates Phase B to every Alex watching. The lifestyle is the pitch.
