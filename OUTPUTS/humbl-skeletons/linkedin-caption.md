# Humbl Skeletons #1 — Anatomy of a converting hero section
## LinkedIn caption (FINAL)

---

Want more demos booked from your hero section?

Then it needs to do these 6 things:

→ One headline. One promise.
If a visitor can't tell what you do in 3 seconds, they leave.

→ One subhead that names who it's for.
Specifics keep the right person on the page. "For early-seed AI founders" filters out tire-kickers and pulls in your actual buyer.

→ One CTA. Verb + outcome.
"Get started" tells me nothing. "Book a free audit" tells me what I get and what happens next. Two CTAs usually split attention so pick one.

→ 3 benefits. Pick the strongest.
Three forces you to choose what actually matters. Always prioritize benefits over features.

→ Show the actual product.
Real interface, real data. Stock illustrations and 3D renders are filler. Visitors trust what they can see working.

→ Real testimonials or client logos.
"Trusted by 100+ teams" with no logos hurts more than helps. One named customer with a face beats ten anonymous quotes.

Run your hero against these 6. If you're missing 3, you've found your conversion leak.

DM "AUDIT" + your URL and I'll send back the 3 biggest leaks in your hero. Free.

## Format spec — use for all Skeleton + Rules posts

This is the template going forward. Every post in the skeletons or rules series follows this structure:

1. **Hook = plain benefit, in a question.** Lead with what the founder already wants (more demos, more signups, more clicks). No metaphors, no jargon like "above the fold." If a non-designer founder doesn't get it in the first 8 words, rewrite.
   - Good: "Want more demos booked from your hero section?"
   - Bad: "Steal this hero section skeleton. Stop losing visitors above the fold."
   - Bad: "Most startup hero sections do 2 jobs well and fail at the other 4."

2. **One-line bridge.** "Then it needs to do these X things:" Commits to a number. Sets up the list.

3. **Each bullet teaches.** Bullet name (rule) + 1-3 short sentences explaining the why or showing a contrast example. Reader should learn even without opening the visual.

4. **Closing diagnostic.** Turn the list into a self-check: "Run your X against these. If you're missing 3, you've found your leak."

5. **CTA.** "DM 'AUDIT' + your URL and I'll send back the 3 biggest leaks in your [thing]. Free."

## Hook variations (for future skeletons / rules)

- "Want more [outcome] from your [thing]? Then it needs to do these X things."
- "More [outcome 1]. More [outcome 2]. Here are the X things your [thing] needs."
- "Your [thing] isn't [outcome] because it's missing 3 of these X things."
