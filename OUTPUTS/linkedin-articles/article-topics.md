## 2. The onboarding mistakes that kill your activation rate

**The search this answers:** "how to improve saas onboarding" / "saas activation rate"

What to cover: empty states that leave users stranded, unclear first action (the activation moment), too many steps before value, missing progress indicators. Include the concept of the "aha moment" — the exact point where a user gets why they signed up. Most founders don't know what theirs is.

**Why it works:** Activation is the most searched product metric after churn. Founders Google this constantly, especially post-launch when signups aren't sticking.

## 3. Your pricing page is probably losing you demos

**The search this answers:** "how to design saas pricing page" / "pricing page best practices"

What to cover: feature comparison tables that overwhelm instead of clarify, no recommended plan, burying the price, missing trust signals (logos, testimonials, money-back language). Strong opinion: most SaaS pricing pages treat the page like a spec sheet instead of a sales conversation.

**Why it works:** Pricing page design is a constant source of founder anxiety. High-intent search. Very citable because the advice is specific.

## 5. How to fix your signup flow without rebuilding your product

**The search this answers:** "saas signup flow best practices" / "reduce signup drop-off"

What to cover: too many fields upfront, no progress indication, unclear error messages, no trust signals during signup (SSL badge, no CC required, etc.), asking for payment before showing value. Strong take: most signup leaks are a 2-hour fix, not a product overhaul.

**Why it works:** Founders with live products search this when they see drop-off in analytics. High intent. Very actionable.
