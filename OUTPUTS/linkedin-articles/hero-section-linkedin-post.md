## Version A — List/framework hook (longer, teaches in-post)

Want to know why your SaaS hero section isn't converting?

It's missing 1 of these 4 things.

I've audited 50+ landing pages and it's almost always the same:

1. A headline that leads with outcome, not feature.
"AI-powered platform for modern teams" describes the product. "Your engineers lose 3 hours a day to status updates" describes the reader. Different planet.

2. A subheadline that adds something new.
Audience and mechanism. Who it's for, how it works. If your subheadline could appear on a competitor's page unchanged, it's not doing its job.

3. One CTA with specific copy.
Two CTAs side by side make the visitor pick. Picking creates friction. And "Get started" is the weakest CTA on the internet. Name what happens after the click.

4. Social proof next to the CTA.
Even one line. "Trusted by 130+ engineering teams" placed where hesitation lives turns almost-decided into decided.

Cover your logo. Show your hero to a stranger. If they can't explain your product in one sentence, that's your conversion leak.
