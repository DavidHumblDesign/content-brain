## LINKEDIN POST — CAROUSEL CAPTION

---

From 50+ landing page audits: most SaaS pages sit at 1-2% conversion.

You spend weeks on them. Then paste in "Built for scale, designed for growth" and wonder why nobody's clicking.

Copy came last. Nobody answered the conversion questions before touching the design.

You can use AI to architect the ideal structure before you open Figma.

The HUMBL framework gives you the structure.

H: Hook.
Feed AI your #1 visitor pain. It writes the headline. You edit it.
A visitor lands and makes a gut call in under 5 seconds: does this solve my problem or not. Weak headline, wrong answer, they're gone. Everything below the fold doesn't exist for them.

U: Urgency.
Give AI your specific ICP. It writes pain bullets that actually land.
"B2B companies" is not an ICP. The more specific you are (role, revenue band, core struggle), the more a visitor reads a pain bullet and thinks "that's me."

M: Mechanism.
Tell AI the outcome, not the feature. It writes the transformation.
"AI-powered lead scoring" says nothing. "Spend 80% less time on leads that go nowhere" makes someone sit up. Visitors care about where they end up, not how the engine works.

B: Believability.
Feed AI your real proof. It formats testimonials that convert.
"Love this tool!" is not proof. Proof is a number, a name, a title, a company. "We cut churn by 30% in 6 weeks" with a real person attached. AI formats the layout. You have to provide the actual evidence.

L: Lower barriers.
Give AI your real objections. It writes FAQs that close.
Most teams skip this section entirely. A good FAQ quietly kills the doubts that stop people right before they convert: will this work for my situation, is the price worth it, what makes you different. Every unanswered question is a visitor who bounced.

Use it to get unstuck, test ideas, or just move faster.

One rule: rewrite everything in your voice before it goes live. AI gives you the structure. The SLOP is your problem to fix. Or just use any anti-AI.md files lol.
