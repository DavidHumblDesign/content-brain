3 months to simplify creative connections.

Castingofka is a Prague-based casting platform for actors, extras, and models in film, TV, and commercial productions. The platform helps creatives register, build portfolios, and connect with producers. It had strong functionality and poor usability.

The brief

The founders knew the platform worked. They wanted to know if it could feel better. The goal was a full redesign: intuitive, consistent, and ready for the next stage of growth.

What was actually broken

The interface was cluttered, the visual style inconsistent, and the overall experience felt dated. Users were switching between a website and a mobile app that behaved like different products: different navigation, different visual language, constant context-switching.

That created confusion and lower engagement. The platform had good functionality buried under an experience that made it hard to use.

The work

A 3-month redesign sprint focused on unifying the experience across web and mobile. Clean layouts, consistent navigation, clear hierarchy throughout. Every component rebuilt with the same design system so the two platforms finally felt like one product.

Worked directly with the internal tech team to make sure the new system was implementable and maintainable, not just a Figma handoff they'd struggle to ship.
