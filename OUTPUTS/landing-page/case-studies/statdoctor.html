The brief

Dr. Anu G came through a free audit offer. StatDoctor connects patients with expert doctors in real time, making healthcare more accessible without the waiting room. The landing page was already live and functional.

The booking rate was disappointing. She wanted it fixed.

What was actually broken

The page lacked visual clarity, a clear hierarchy, and a logical flow. Visitors arrived curious and left without taking action. They didn't understand the value fast enough, didn't know what to click, and rarely reached the booking form.

For a service operating in healthcare (a high-trust category), that's a serious problem. The page wasn't building trust before asking for a booking.

The work

A focused 5-day redesign sprint. Rebuilt the page around visual clarity and a logical user flow, with one organising principle for every section: book a consultation.

The messaging was restructured to lead with outcomes, not features. Trust signals were moved earlier in the page. The CTA was placed where visitors were actually ready to act.
