The brief

Emmanuel Gatwech needed product mockups for investors and early users. Fast. The goal was to show the product clearly enough that investors could grasp the value and back it.

The core of Sahil's product is a ranking algorithm that gathers and evaluates products from various suppliers. The backend logic was solid. Getting that into a UI a business owner could actually use was the hard part.

What was actually broken

The existing product concept had no UX structure and no visual hierarchy. There was no clear flow between user actions, and the interface didn't communicate what the product could do.

Early users were confused. Investors couldn't grasp the value. A strong backend idea was getting lost before anyone saw what it was capable of.

The work

A focused 24-hour sprint. Short alignment call with Emmanuel to understand the core product flow, then straight into Figma. The brief was clear: clean, minimal UI that makes the product story easy to follow.

The hardest part was translating the ranking algorithm into something a business owner could actually interact with: supplier comparisons, product selection logic, and the reasons behind each recommendation, presented without feeling like a data dump.

Iterated directly with Emmanuel throughout the day. By the end of it: investor-ready mockups that read as a real product.
