The brief

When Augment's co-founders reached out, the product was gaining traction. Design wasn't keeping up. Their development team was handling the visuals: no structured design process, no dedicated ownership, no system for consistency.

The product worked. It didn't feel cohesive or intuitive. User journeys were fragmented, visual hierarchy was unclear, and the interface didn't reflect the sophistication of a FinTech company preparing to raise capital.

What was actually broken

There was no design foundation. Developers were making visual decisions on the fly, which created inconsistency across the product over time. Each new feature added more noise.

For a platform where investors are moving real money, that matters a lot. The interface needs to communicate trust and precision. What they had communicated neither.

The work

Rebuilt the web app from the ground up. Built a visual system grounded in clarity and credibility, with structure and hierarchy that made the product feel like a serious FinTech platform.

Worked directly with the founders and internal developers throughout, adapting to their workflow rather than adding process overhead. As the product grew, the scope grew with it. New features, new pages, ongoing iteration as user feedback came in.

What started as a redesign project became a standing arrangement. Four years in, I already know the product and the founders' thinking. That's faster than onboarding someone new every time something needs building.
