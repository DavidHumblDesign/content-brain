12 — FAQ

Common questions

How long does a landing page take?
First designs in 72 hours. A full design plus Webflow build is usually 10 to 14 days, depending on revision rounds. If you have a hard deadline, tell me on day one. I'll work backward from it.
What do you need from me to get started?
A short brief (I send a questionnaire), your logo and brand assets if you have them, and a few links to sites you like. If you already have copy written, I'll use it. If not, I'll tell you what each section needs to say. Actually writing the copy is on you though. I don't do copywriting.
I just need a few small changes to my existing page. Do you take that?
For small fixes, the free audit is where to start. I'll tell you exactly what to change and you can implement it yourself or hand it to your developer. For anything more involved on an existing site, the retainer makes more sense. I don't do hourly work.
Do you work with pre-revenue companies?
Yes. Most of my clients are seed-stage or earlier. Some are pre-launch. The landing page is often the thing that gets you to revenue in the first place. I'm open to sweat equity arrangements for the right company. Ask me directly and we can talk about it.
Design-only versus design plus Webflow build. What's the difference?
Design-only means you get Figma files and your team builds it. Design plus Webflow means I build it too. If you have developers on another stack, design-only is fine. If you want it live fast without adding to anyone's sprint, go with the full package.
Why Webflow and not something else?
It's what I build fastest in. It's also genuinely the right tool for most early-stage startup sites: fast to update, CMS-ready, and no developer needed for small changes after launch. If your stack requires something else, I'll deliver design-only and your team can build it in whatever they use.
